Proposal A — Workspace with sidebar

The app grows from a settings page into a desktop workspace: a left rail switches between Dictate, Meetings, Voices, Models, History and Settings. Meetings is a three-pane editor (archive · transcript · speakers + voice map) with a per-speaker replay bar. The browser extension becomes a thin capture device with a small side panel that mirrors the live transcript; editing happens here. Window ≈ 1120×740 (from 700×860).
